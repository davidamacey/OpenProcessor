# Roadmap and work in progress

Everything else in this site describes behaviour that exists on the 0.7.0
branch. The items below are in progress and are **not** shipped; do not plan on
them yet.

- **Decode once on the GPU.** One decode per photo shared by every stage.
- **PP-OCRv6.** The next text-recognition model generation.
- **Crop sizing.** Choosing crop sizes per model input.
- **Plate text.** Reading registration plates in the region cascade.
- **GPU speed.** Further ingest and inference throughput work, measured against
  the recorded baseline in `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` before any claim is made.

Progress is tracked on the project's issue tracker and in the changelog.
